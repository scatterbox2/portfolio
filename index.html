My Approach

I follow a research-first approach to make sure product information is accurate, organized, and ready for marketplace listing.

🔍

1. Research

Research the product using available supplier information and reliable online sources to identify specifications, features, materials, dimensions, compatibility, and other relevant details.

✓

2. Verify & Enrich

Verify important product information, fill in missing details, and organize the findings into structured product data.

📊

3. Analyze

Review competitor listings, pricing, product features, customer feedback, and relevant keywords to identify opportunities for stronger listing content.

📝

4. Optimize

Turn the research into clear, organized, and SEO-focused titles, bullet points, descriptions, and listing information.

🛒

5. Prepare for Listing

Adapt and organize the completed product information for the requirements and formatting of the target e-commerce marketplace.

✓

6. Quality Check

Review the final listing for accuracy, consistency, formatting, spelling, completeness, and missing information before submission.
